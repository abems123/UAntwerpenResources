$\displaystyle\int \dfrac{1-4x}{2x^2+5x+2}dx$\\

$2x^2+5x+2 = \left(2x+1\right)\left(x+2\right)$\\

$\dfrac{1-4x}{\left(2x+1\right)\left(x+2\right)} = \dfrac{A}{2x+1} + \dfrac{B}{x+2}$\\

$A = \left. \dfrac{1-4x}{x+2}\right|_{x=-\tfrac{1}{2}}=2$\\

$B = \left. \dfrac{1-4x}{2x+1}\right|_{x=-2}=-3$\\

$\Rightarrow \displaystyle\int \left(\dfrac{2}{2x+1} + \dfrac{-3}{x+2}\right)dx$\\

$= \displaystyle\int \dfrac{2dx}{2x+1} -3\displaystyle\int\dfrac{dx}{x+2}$\\

$= \ln{\left|2x+1\right|}-3\ln{\left|x+2\right|}+k$\\
