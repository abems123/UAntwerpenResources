$f(x)=\operatorname{Bgsin}\left(2x^2-1\right)$\\

$Domf=\{x\in \mathbb{R} | -1\leq2x^2-1\leq1\}$\\

$=\{x\in \mathbb{R} | -1\leq x\leq1\} = [-1,1]$\\

$f'(x)=\dfrac{\left(2x^2-1\right)'}{\sqrt{1-\left(2x^2-1\right)^2}}$\\

$=\dfrac{4x}{\sqrt{1-\left(4x^4-4x^2+1\right)}}$\\

$=\dfrac{4x}{\sqrt{4x^2-4x^4}}$\\

$=\dfrac{4x}{\sqrt{4x^2\left(1-x^2\right)}}$\\

$=\dfrac{4x}{2|x|\sqrt{\left(1-x^2\right)}}$\\

$f'(x)=\begin{cases}
\dfrac{2}{\sqrt{1-x^2}}, & 0<x<1\\
-\dfrac{2}{\sqrt{1-x^2}}, & -1<x<0\\
\end{cases}$
