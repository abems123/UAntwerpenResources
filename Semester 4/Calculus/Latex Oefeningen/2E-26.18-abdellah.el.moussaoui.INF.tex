$\log_5x+4\log_x5=5$ \\

$\Rightarrow \log_5x+\log_x625=5$\\

$\Rightarrow \dfrac{\ln{x}}{\ln5} + \dfrac{\ln625}{\ln{x}} = 5$\\

$\Rightarrow \dfrac{(\ln{x})^2}{\ln5} + \ln625 - 5\ln{x} = 0$\\

$\Rightarrow (\ln{x})^2 - 5\ln5\ln{x} + \ln625\ln5 = 0$\\

$\Rightarrow (\ln{x})^2 - 5\ln5\ln{x} + 4(\ln5)^2 = 0$\\

Laat $Y = \ln{x}$\\

$\Rightarrow Y^2 - 5\ln5Y + 4(\ln5)^2 = 0$\\

$\Rightarrow (Y-\ln5)(Y-4\ln5)=0$\\

$\Rightarrow Y=\ln5 $ of $ Y=4\ln5$\\

$\Rightarrow \ln{x}=\ln5 $ of $ \ln{x}=4\ln5$\\

$\Rightarrow \boxed{x=5 , x=625}$\\
