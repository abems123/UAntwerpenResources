$T_6(f,1)(x)$ voor $f(x) = x^6+x^3-x^2+4$ \\

$T_n(f,a)(x) = \displaystyle\sum_{k = 0}^{n} \dfrac{f^{(k)}(a)}{k!}(x - a)^k$ \\

$T_6(f,1)(x) = \displaystyle\sum_{k = 0}^{6} \dfrac{f^k(1)}{k!}(x - 1)^k$ \\

$f^1(x)=\left(x^6+x^3-x^2+4\right)' = 6x^5+3x^2-2x$ \\

$\Rightarrow f^1(1)=7$\\

$f^2(x)=\left(x^6+x^3-x^2+4\right)'' = 30x^4+6x-2$ \\

$\Rightarrow f^2(1)=34$\\

$f^3(x)=\left(x^6+x^3-x^2+4\right)''' = 120x^3+6$\\

$\Rightarrow f^3(1)=126$\\

$f^4(x)=\left(x^6+x^3-x^2+4\right)^{iv} = 360x^2$ \\

$\Rightarrow f^4(1)=360$\\

$f^5(x)=\left(x^6+x^3-x^2+4\right)^{v} = 720x$ \\

$\Rightarrow f^5(1)=720$\\

$f^6(x)=\left(x^6+x^3-x^2+4\right)^{vi} = 720$ \\

$\Rightarrow f^6(1)=720$\\
