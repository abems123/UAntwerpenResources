$\displaystyle\int \dfrac{x-1}{x^2}\ln xdx$\\

$=\displaystyle\int \dfrac{\ln x}{x}dx-\displaystyle\int \dfrac{\ln x}{x^2}dx$\\

$=\dfrac{1}{2}\ln^2x+c-\displaystyle\int \dfrac{\ln x}{x^2}dx$\\

Laat $u=\ln x$ , $dv=\dfrac{1}{x^2}dx$\\

$\Rightarrow du=\dfrac{1}{x}dx$ , $v=-\dfrac{1}{x}$\\

$\Rightarrow \displaystyle\int \dfrac{\ln x}{x^2}dx
=uv-\displaystyle\int vdu$\\

$=-\dfrac{\ln x}{x}-\displaystyle\int \left(-\dfrac{1}{x}\right)\dfrac{1}{x}dx$\\

$=-\dfrac{\ln x}{x}+\displaystyle\int \dfrac{1}{x^2}dx$\\

$=-\dfrac{\ln x}{x}-\dfrac{1}{x} +c$\\

$\Rightarrow \displaystyle\int \dfrac{x-1}{x^2}\ln xdx
=\dfrac{1}{2}\ln^2x-\left(-\dfrac{\ln x}{x}-\dfrac{1}{x}\right)+c$\\

$=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{2}\ln^2x+\dfrac{\ln x+1}{x}+c}$\\
