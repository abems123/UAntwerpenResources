# Best Exercises from Oefeningenreeks 2X for the Oefeningen Exam

This document gives the **highest-yield exercises from Oefeningenreeks 2X** based on the old uploaded exams, with a focus on the kinds of questions that **keep coming back**.

It also includes the **most important theory questions** from chapter 2 that matter for the theory part of the exam.

---

## 1. Best Chapter-2 Exercise Sets to Prioritize

After comparing the chapter 2 exercise sets with the old exams, the most useful ones are:

### Highest priority
1. **Oefeningenreeks 2D**
2. **Oefeningenreeks 2E**
3. **Oefeningenreeks 2B**

### Lower priority, but still useful
4. **Oefeningenreeks 2C**
5. **Oefeningenreeks 2F**

### Lowest priority from 2X
6. **Oefeningenreeks 2A**

---

## 2. Why These Are the Most Important

The chapter 2 sets are:

- **2A**: continuity
- **2B**: cyclometric functions
- **2C**: limits
- **2D**: calculating limits
- **2E**: exponential and logarithmic functions
- **2F**: hyperbolic functions

From the exam patterns, the questions that come back the most are:

- **limit calculations**
- **exponential/logarithmic equations**
- **inverse trigonometric proof-style questions**

That is why **2D**, **2E**, and **2B** are the most important.

---

## 3. Oefeningenreeks 2D — The Most Important One

This is the **most repeated chapter-2 exam family**.

On many exams, there is a block of **three limits without using L’Hôpital**, usually involving:

- factorization
- standard trigonometric limits
- exponential-type limits such as expressions of the form `(1 + ...)^(...)`

### These old exams look like 2D

- **2022–2023 1st sit, question 4**
- **2022–2023 2nd sit, question 4**
- **2023–2024 1st sit, question 4**
- **2024–2025 1st sit, question 4**
- **2024–2025 2nd sit, question 4**

### Best 2D exercises to do

- **2D 14.11 – 14.24**
- **2D 15.1 – 15.6**
- **2D 16.1 – 16.10**
- **2D 19.\***
- **2D 22**

### Why these matter
These are the exercises that train exactly the kind of limit manipulation that keeps returning on the oefeningen exams.

## 5. Oefeningenreeks 2B — Inverse Trig Proof Questions

This set is not as frequent as 2D and 2E, but when it appears, it often appears in a **very similar form**.

These questions usually ask you to prove that a complicated sum of inverse trigonometric expressions is equal to a nice angle such as:

- `π/4`
- `π/6`

### These old exams look like 2B

- **2018–2019 2nd sit, question 4**
- **2021–2022 1st sit, question 3**
- **2022–2023 2nd sit, question 3**

### Best 2B exercises to do

- **2B 6.2**
- **2B 6.4**
- **2B 6.8**
- **2B 6.10**
- **2B 6.11**
- **2B 6.12**
- **2B 8.1 – 8.4**
- **2B 10 – 11**

### Why these matter
These are the best practice for the classic exam pattern:

- simplify inverse trig expressions
- use identities cleverly
- end with a simple exact angle

## 6. Oefeningenreeks 2C — Mostly Important for Theory Support

For the oefeningen exam itself, **2C is less important than 2D and 2E**.

But it matters more for **theory**, because chapter 2 theory can ask about:

- limits
- one-sided limits
- improper limits
- infinite limits
- limits at infinity

### Best 2C exercises to do

- **2C 1.1 – 1.4**
- **2C 9 – 10**

### Why these matter
These help you understand:

- removable discontinuities
- left and right limits
- how to speak formally about limits
- the kind of definitions that are useful for the theory exam

## 7. Oefeningenreeks 2F — Useful, but Not a Top Priority

I would **not** spend much time on 2F before finishing 2D, 2E, and 2B.

Still, it can help for theory or formula knowledge related to hyperbolic functions.

### Best 2F exercises to do

- **2F 1**
- **2F 2**
- **2F 3.1 – 3.3**

### Why these matter
They train standard identities and formulas such as:

- formulas for `cosh(x-y)`, `sinh(x-y)`, `tanh(x-y)`
- triple-angle formulas
- identities involving `tanh`, `sech`, etc.

## 9. Theory Questions You Must Know from Chapter 2

The theory part is important because it counts for **8/20** of the total score.

From the Encyclopedia page and the chapter 2 material, the most important chapter-2-looking theory question is:

### Most important theory question
- **Definition of an improper limit (oneigenlijke limiet)**

You should know this **formally**, not only intuitively.

### Also important to know
You should clearly know the difference between:

- ordinary finite limits
- infinite limits
- limits at infinity
- one-sided limits
- improper limits

These are exactly the kinds of definitions that are easy to test in theory.

## 10. Theory Questions from the Encyclopedia Page That Matter Most

### Strongest chapter-2 match
- **Definition of an improper limit / oneigenlijke limiet**

### Also very important overall, but more chapter 3 than chapter 2
- **The three mean value theorems + a smart application such as L’Hôpital**
- **Convex / concave proof questions**

So if you are focusing only on **chapter 2 theory**, the one you should absolutely memorize first is:

- the **formal definitions of improper limits**
- and the distinctions between the different types of limits
