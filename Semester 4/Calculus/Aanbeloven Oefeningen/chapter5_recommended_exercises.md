# Chapter 5 — Recommended Exercises for the Oefeningen Exam

This file is a practical Chapter 5 study guide based on the **course exercise sets (5A–5M)** and the **old oefeningen exams**.

The goal is not to do everything, but to focus on the exercises that are closest to the recurring exam patterns.

---

## Big picture

For Chapter 5, the oefeningen exam mostly comes back to these patterns:

1. **Oneigenlijke integralen**  
   Especially integrals with a singular point or an infinite boundary.

2. **Oppervlakten**  
   Areas between curves, polar areas, and parametric loop areas.

3. **Omwentelingsvolumes**  
   Volumes around the X-axis or Y-axis, sometimes with parametric curves.

4. **Booglengtes**  
   Cartesian, parametric, and sometimes polar arc length.

5. **Numerieke integratie**  
   Less common in full exams, but it appears in model questions.

## Best order to study them

### 1. Oneigenlijke integralen

Do these first:

- **5B-3.3**
- **5B-5.4**
- **5B-1.6**
- **5B-5.8**
- **5B-5.12**

Why: this is the easiest exam pattern to recognize, but also easy to mess up if you forget to split at singular points.

---

### 2. Oppervlakten

Do these next:

- **5D-4.3**
- **5D-4.10**
- **5E-1**
- **5F-1.5**
- **5F-1.10**
- **5F-3.6**

Why: area questions are very common, especially between curves or in polar coordinates.

---

### 3. Omwentelingsvolumes

Then do:

- **5G-1.12**
- **5G-6.8**
- **5H-1**

Why: volume questions usually look scary, but most of them are just choosing the correct formula and interval.

---

### 4. Booglengtes

Then do:

- **5I-1.3**
- **5I-2.3**
- **5J-2.3**
- **5K-1.4** or **5K-1.5**

Why: arc length appeared in several exams, and the main difficulty is simplifying the square root.

---

### 5. Extra if you still have time

Only after the main list:

- **5C-1**
- **5L-1.3**
- **5M-1**

Why: these are useful, but they are less central than improper integrals, areas, volumes, and arc lengths.

---

## What to skip if time is short

You can skip most of **5A**, except maybe:

- **5A-2.4**

Why: a floor-function style question appeared in **2024 z2 Q9**, but most of 5A is less representative of the exam level.

Also skip most very easy direct area/volume questions like:

- **5D-1**
- **5G-1.1**

unless you need a warmup.

The exam level is usually closer to:

> Find intersections / choose limits / use symmetry / then integrate.
