# Chapter 4 — Recommended Exercises for the Oefeningen Exam

This file is a practical Chapter 4 study guide based on the **course exercise set 4A** and the **old oefeningen exams** you uploaded.

Chapter 4 in the notes covers:

- **4.2** primitive functions and integrals
- **4.3** integration by splitting
- **4.4** integration by parts
- **4.5** substitution
- **4.6** mixed examples
- **4.7** partial fractions
- **4.8** the rule of Fuss
- **4.9** integrals of rationally composed trigonometric functions
- **4.10** integrals of irrationally composed functions :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

The goal is not to do everything, but to do the exercises that are **most similar to the recurring exam patterns**.

---

## Big picture

For Chapter 4, the oefeningen exam keeps coming back to a fairly small group of patterns:

1. **Choosing the correct integration technique**
2. **Integration by parts**
3. **Substitution**
4. **Partial fractions**
5. **Standard arctan / arcsin forms**
6. **Radical integrals** like `\sqrt{a^2-x^2}`, `\sqrt{x^2+a^2}`, `1/\sqrt{...}`
7. **Mixed “harder single integrals”** where you must first recognize the right form

So the best strategy is:

- first master **partial fractions**
- then the strongest **substitution** blocks
- then the strongest **integration by parts** blocks
- then do the **standard quadratic / arctan / arcsin** forms
- then do the **irrational/radical** blocks
- leave the very basic first exercises for last

# Priority 1 — Absolutely do these

## Partial fractions and Fuss-style decomposition

These are among the most exam-like Chapter 4 exercises of all.

### Must do

- **4A.42.1–4A.42.6**
- **4A.43.1–4A.43.15**
- **4A.44.1–4A.44.14**

Why these matter:
- These train the exact exam pattern where you must decompose a rational function and integrate the result.
- This is one of the most recurring Chapter 4 question styles.
- `4A.43` and `4A.44` are especially strong because they contain the more realistic exam-level rational functions. 

### Why this block is so important

Chapter 4 explicitly builds toward:

- **splitsen in partieelbreuken**
- **de regel van Fuss** 

So if you want exam efficiency, this is one of the first things to master.

## Strong substitution blocks

### Must do

- **4A.27.1–4A.27.17**
- **4A.28.1–4A.28.6**
- **4A.32.1–4A.32.8**
- **4A.33.1–4A.33.8**
- **4A.34.1–4A.34.10**
- **4A.35.1–4A.35.6**

Why:
- These are the exact kind of substitution questions that keep coming back:
  - derivative-of-the-denominator patterns
  - quadratic substitution patterns
  - exponential substitution
  - composed exponential/trig forms
  - non-`e` exponential integrals
- These are very close to the standard Chapter 4 exam integral style. 

### Especially high-yield inside this group

- **4A.27**
- **4A.33**
- **4A.34**

Why:
- `4A.27` is the classic “spot the inside derivative” block.
- `4A.33–34` are the more exam-like substitution problems where the inside is exponential or trigonometric, so the trick is less obvious.

# Priority 2 — Very high yield

## Integration by parts

### Must do

- **4A.14.1–4A.14.13**
- **4A.15.1–4A.15.11**
- **4A.18.1–4A.18.16**
- **4A.19.1–4A.19.5**

Why:
- These are exactly the recurring Chapter 4 patterns where you must recognize that the integral is a product and choose `u` and `dv` well.
- `4A.15` gives the standard polynomial times exponential type.
- `4A.18` gives the standard polynomial times trigonometric type.
- `4A.19` gives the famous exponential-times-trig type that is very exam-like. 

### Also recommended

- **4A.16.1–4A.16.2**
- **4A.17**

Why:
- These are smaller, but they train the idea that one integral can often be solved in multiple ways.
- That helps a lot in the exam when you hesitate between substitution and parts.

## Standard quadratic / arctan blocks

### Must do

- **4A.36.1–4A.36.15**
- **4A.37.1–4A.37.14**
- **4A.39.1–4A.39.10**

Why:
- These are the classic forms leading to `\arctan`, especially after completing the square.
- This is one of the safest Chapter 4 patterns to prepare because it appears again and again inside harder questions too. 

### Why these matter so much

Even when the exam question is not “just” an arctan integral, many harder rational integrals reduce to these exact standard forms.

# Priority 3 — Very useful and often examined

## Irrational / radical integrals

### Must do

- **4A.40.1–4A.40.13**
- **4A.41.1–4A.41.13**

Why:
- These are the standard Chapter 4 radical forms:
  - `1/\sqrt{a^2-x^2}`
  - `1/\sqrt{x^2+a^2}`
  - `1/\sqrt{x^2-a^2}`
  - shifted quadratics under the square root
- These are very exam-relevant because they test whether you recognize the right transformation immediately. 

### Also recommended

- **4A.29.1–4A.29.4**
- **4A.31.1–4A.31.15**

Why:
- These are good bridge exercises between basic substitution and the heavier irrational forms.
- They help you become comfortable with roots before the harder Chapter 4 radical questions.

# Final advice

If you want to be truly safe for the oefeningen exam, do **not** spread your time equally over all of 4A.

The highest return comes from mastering:

- **partial fractions**
- **substitution**
- **integration by parts**
- **standard arctan / arcsin forms**
- **radical integrals**

That is where Chapter 4 questions usually live.

Also, for Chapter 4, success is not only about calculation speed — it is very often about **recognizing the right technique quickly**.
